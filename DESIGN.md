Responsive typography and gutters use clamp().

Radii follow one proportional rule: `min(8px, max(1px, 1.6% of the rendered item's shorter side))`. CSS expresses this with responsive size tokens grouped by component: large surfaces/cards use up to 8px (`--radius-surface`), medium 16:9 media uses up to 5px (`--radius-media`), and full-width media can reach 8px (`--radius-media-large`). Small controls such as a 137×49 button stay at 1px (`--radius-control`). This gives a 1200×500 card 8px, a 580×326 image about 5px, and that button about 1px; `clamp()` scales card and media radii down on narrower screens. Use the token matching the rendered component, never copy a large card radius onto a small control.
